# Risk Register Dashboard

An interactive risk register built to demonstrate proactive risk management 
across cross-functional product and engineering teams.

## Live demo
👉 https://varikutiteja.github.io/risk-register

## What this shows
- Visual probability vs impact matrix with color coded severity zones
- 8 real project risks across Security, Compliance, Engineering and Growth
- Click any risk to see full detail, owner, mitigation plan and score
- Summary metrics — total, critical, high and mitigated risks

## Risk severity framework

| Score | Severity | Color |
|---|---|---|
| 16–25 | Critical | Red |
| 9–15 | High | Amber |
| 4–8 | Medium | Blue |
| 1–3 | Low | Green |

## PM skills demonstrated
- Risk identification and categorization
- Probability and impact scoring
- Mitigation planning
- Cross-functional ownership assignment
